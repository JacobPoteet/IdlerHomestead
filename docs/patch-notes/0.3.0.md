# Idler Homestead 0.3.0 (pre-release)

A settings-and-sound update with one big simplification: wood is now the only fuel. Plans now start work on their own, objects can be picked up and moved, crops are readable at a glance, and the world pauses properly when you do.

> **Saves from 0.2.0 can't be loaded.** The save format changed again (and the wood change rewrote stored resources). An old save shows a prompt where you can delete it or keep it.

## Highlights
- **One kind of wood.** No more green firewood, seasoned firewood or splitting. Fell trees, store wood, burn wood. Spring and fall need a little warmth, winter needs a lot, summer needs none.
- **Plan means do it.** Planned tiles are tilled and planted straight away, even with the Routine off, and tilling no longer waits for seeds.
- **Pick up and move.** Any building you placed can be picked up or moved from its card.
- **Settings, with UI size.** Choose a smaller or larger interface, window options and visual comfort settings.
- **Sound.** The world now has ambience, effects and volume sliders. All sounds are placeholders for now and easy to replace.

## New features
### Wood and warmth
- Wood is the only fuel. The chopping block, the split job and seasoning are gone.
- The woodshed is dry storage and is needed to raise the wood limit above the starting cap.
- Warmth need follows the year: none in summer, partial in spring and fall (an uncovered night costs health), full in winter, scaled by winter severity, cold snaps and your shelter.
- One wood chip in the top bar and one wood row in the Pantry, showing stock, tonight's burn and how many nights it lasts.

### Planning and the homesteader
- **Plan automates the plot.** Planned tiles are tilled and planted at once, and the homesteader finishes the whole plot after any other job. Tilling happens even when you are out of seeds, and planting follows when seeds arrive.
- **Tip cards pause the game** until you dismiss them.
- A "Nothing to do" nudge appears when the Routine is on and finds no work, and the Routine button pulses while it applies.
- **Double-tap Plan** on touch to keep it on for several plans.

### Building
- **Pick up and Move** on any placed object's card. Moves use the same preview as placing, soil under a pick-up returns to its old state, and risky cases (a running smoker, a stocked woodshed, a pen with animals) are refused with a reason.
- A finished batch stuck in a full station can be discarded from the station's card.

### Settings
- **UI size:** Auto plus fixed steps, applied immediately, capped to what your window fits.
- **Window (desktop):** fullscreen, vsync, remember window size and position.
- **Visual comfort:** reduce motion, film grain, colour grade strength, ambient and weather effects.
- **Idle takeover delay:** half, normal, double or four times.

### Sound
- Ambience (birds, creek, wind, crickets, rain, fire crackle, power hum), effects for work, fishing, the truck and alerts, and thunder in storms.
- Master, Effects, Ambience and Music sliders, mute when unfocused, and a **Listen to every sound** screen (Settings, or F7).
- Drop a file into `game/assets/audio/<bus>/<cue>.ogg` to replace any placeholder.

### Rescue and later runs
- The rescue scene says what failed (cold or hunger) and where you were, and points at the next shelter upgrade.
- On your first rescue the recovery scene suggests one first change. Run 2 gets short cards about what carried over.

## Improvements
- **Crops are readable.** Each growth stage has an outline and a distinct shape, thirsty crops show a droplet, ripe crops sparkle, and planned tiles show a dashed frame with a faint ghost sprout. Crops stay visible at night.
- **Effects follow game time.** Flames, clouds, wind, rain and mist stop when you pause and speed up with 2x and 4x. Sleep fast-forward is capped so flames don't strobe.
- **Winter readiness** now counts the warmth your electric heaters will make.
- **A truck-ready marker** bobs over the truck when a trip is ready.
- **Food:** you now eat what would spoil first.
- **Lists:** Page Up/Down, Home/End and coasting after a fast drag. The Routine panel scrolls.
- **Pantry rows** keep touch-sized spacing.
- The title screen shows the build tag and commit hash, and the out-of-date save prompt shows the build too.
- The **Stand cooler** line now says what it really does (+4 items a day).

## Bug fixes
- Cancelling a placement can no longer destroy crops under the footprint.
- The "Full:" pill now lists only stores that are actually holding something back.
- The Routine hint is no longer lost when a panel covers the world.

## Performance
- The game allocates about a tenth as much memory per tick, and idle job searching is shared between chores.
- Saves are about two thirds smaller (a mid-game save went from roughly 300 KB to 100 KB) and load about three times faster.
- The F3 overlay shows sim and draw times and flags anything over budget.

## Under the hood
- GDD decisions D91 through D104. Save version 8.
- Release notes are now hand-written per release in `docs/patch-notes/`.
- Test suite now covers 758 checks.

## Known issues
- All sounds are placeholders and have not been tuned by ear.
- Phone performance and shader cost are still unmeasured.
- Wood balance (curve, caps, how much to stockpile) is untuned (#251).
- Key rebinding and audio polish are not built yet (#237, #243).
- The message log still starts empty each time you open a slot (#224).
- Some older screenshots in the docs show the old two-chip firewood UI (#203).

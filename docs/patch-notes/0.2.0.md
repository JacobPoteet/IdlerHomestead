# Idler Homestead 0.2.0 (pre-release)

A big quality-of-life and atmosphere update. Clearer interface, a real shelter ladder, a rebuilt Journal, a full weather and lighting pass, and a lot of small fixes found in playtesting.

> **Saves from 0.1.0 can't be loaded.** The save format changed. An old save shows a prompt where you can delete it or keep it. New saves are stamped with a version so this will be explicit in future.

## Highlights
- **Build more freely.** Place buildings on any grass or tilled tile. Trees, rocks and crops in the way are cleared first, with a preview and a confirmation.
- **A way up from the truck.** Camp and Shack shelters with real insulation, heater slots and better rest.
- **A Journal that fits your screen.** Chapters in an overview, one fitted chapter at a time.
- **The land feels alive.** Rain and wet ground, snow on roofs, frost, cloud shadows, wind, morning mist, night lights, fireflies and more.
- **Clearer everything.** Tap any top-bar chip to see what it means. Tip cards point at what they talk about. A message log keeps what you missed.

## New features
### Building and land
- **Untill** tool turns tilled soil back to grass.
- **Place on any grass or tilled tile.** Roads and water stay off limits.
- **Clear first.** Placing over a tree, rock, log, brush or junk shows an amber preview and a card listing what will be cleared and which crops are removed. The homesteader clears it, then the building goes up. Cancelling keeps your crops until the last moment.

### Shelter
- **Camp** (lean-to) and **Shack**, unlocked in the Journal, each with an insulation bonus (less warmth needed each night), faster recovery on a fed, warm day, and a heater slot: a stove or heater near your bed counts in full, farther away at reduced strength.
- The Shack adds a desk (study bonus while you sleep there) and extra firewood storage.
- Object cards show insulation, rest and heater-slot tiles.

### Journal
- **Chapter overview** grouped by area (Farm, Food, Home, Know-how), with known / idea / locked counts and "n to study" per chapter.
- **Jump chips** to your study queue and to ideas you can study, plus a Known / Ideas / Locked filter.
- Tapping a chapter fits it to the screen with no panning. Unlock hints on locked ideas.

### Food and the truck
- **Shelf-life spoilage.** Food spoils per batch by days instead of a fixed nightly rate.
- **Keep targets and an explicit Load the truck** replace the sell crate. Set how much of each good you keep, and choose what rides to town.
- The truck panel at home shows trip numbers, time to charge, and a "trip ready" notice.
- Town trips pause game time while you shop.

### Message log
- Every toast is kept in a log (Log button next to Menu, or tap a toast). Repeats show a count and multi-item events merge into one line.
- Events raised while you sleep are held until you wake, in order.

## Improvements
- **Interface clarity:** tap-to-explain HUD chips, clearer Routine / Plan / Fish labels, Journal unlock hints, a tool palette that scales on small screens.
- **Tip cards** point at their target with a pulsing ring, the Tips list marks the ones you've seen, and a new first-run step shows you the top-bar chips.
- **Early game:** a "Where money comes from" tip when you clear your first junk pile, a Catalog hint when you can't afford anything, and a note when the routine plans your starter field.
- **Starter field** is now a compact block that the homesteader can reach.
- **Lists scroll everywhere** (Catalog, Pantry, Fish guide, town menu, Power, tool palette) with a shared scroll bar, drag, wheel, arrow and WASD keys.
- **Health warning** turns amber when your condition is falling, not only when it is low.
- **Plan** switches itself off after one plan (hold Shift on a keyboard to keep it on).

## Visual and atmosphere
- **Weather:** snow now moves with the land, rain scales with zoom and splashes on the ground, wet ground darkens and dries, rain rings on the creek.
- **Winter:** snow on roofs and objects, frost creeping in from the creek.
- **Sky and light:** drifting cloud shadows, morning mist, and warm night lights from the campfire, stove and lamp.
- **Life:** wind sways crops, brush and trees; fireflies on summer evenings; dust motes on dry, windy days; heat shimmer on hot noons and above fires.
- **Selection** uses a clean pixel outline with corner brackets.

## Bug fixes
- The placement confirmation card no longer covers the thing you're placing.
- Cancelling a placement order no longer destroys crops under it.
- The Power panel scrolls and its numbers refresh while paused.
- Late-night news no longer expires unseen while you sleep.
- Several cut-off lists (Pantry, Fish guide, town load list) now scroll instead of hiding content.

## Under the hood
- Save slots are version-stamped, with a clear out-of-date prompt.
- New screenshot driver scenarios and a balance harness that runs in parallel.
- GDD decisions D72 through D90 document every change above.

## Known issues
- Shader performance has not been measured on a phone GPU.
- The message log starts empty each time you open a slot.
- The sleeping spot is chosen automatically (best reachable shelter).

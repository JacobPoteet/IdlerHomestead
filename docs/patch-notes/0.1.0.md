# Idler Homestead 0.1.0 (pre-release)

The first playable build. Wake up beside a dead truck on a messy plot of land, clear it, farm, fish, preserve food, and try to survive the first winter. When you don't, your Field Journal remembers what you learned and you start again on new land.

## Highlights
- **The full loop works.** Clear the land, farm and fish, survive (or not) the winter, get rescued, learn from the Journal and start a new run.
- **Active or idle, your choice.** Play by tapping, or let the homesteader take over with a priority list you can reorder and set conditions on.
- **Knowledge is what you keep.** Gear, money and land reset. Notes, ideas and perks in the Field Journal persist between runs.
- **Hands-on to hands-off.** Power, water, machines and animals take chores off your plate as you grow.
- **Runs on desktop and phone.** Mouse, touch and keyboard, with a layout that adapts to the window.

## New features
### The homestead
- A different messy plot every run: trees, rocks, logs, brush, junk, a creek and a road.
- Click or tap to act. The homesteader walks to the job, with a progress ring for the current action.
- Clear land for wood, stone and a few dollars; till, plant, water and harvest crops.
- Day and night with seasonal sunrise and sunset. The homesteader sleeps at night, and you can skip the rest of the night. Days are long and fast-forward while you sleep.
- Game speed button: paused, 1x, 2x and 4x.

### Fishing and food
- A tension-based fishing mini-game, a fish guide to complete, and ice fishing in winter.
- Fish and produce spoil. A smoker, a freezer and the roadside stand help you keep and sell what you catch.
- Food rules and an automatic pantry decide what you eat, keep and sell.

### Winter and failure
- Warmth is a nightly need in the cold months: burn firewood in a campfire or wood stove, or use an electric heater.
- Frost, a frozen creek, a winter forecast revealed in pieces, and cold snaps that grow harder each year.
- Your condition drops when you go hungry or cold. If it runs out, you are found and rescued, see what the run taught you, and head out to new land.

### The Field Journal
- Notes are written by things you do. Notes unlock ideas. Study ideas at night to learn them.
- A map of everything you know, with cards for each item showing what it does and what you need to unlock it.
- Perks give you a head start in later runs: starting gear, head starts and habits.

### Economy
- A catalog with delivery the next morning, and a truck that charges before you can drive to town.
- Sell goods in town, buy seeds, tools and equipment. Weekly specials change what is on offer.
- A roadside stand sells a capped amount each day from your stock.
- Finds: a daily offer where you pick one of three discoveries.

### Power, water and machines
- A power network: panels, wind, a battery bank and a generator, with a priority list for what gets power. The truck is a battery on the network.
- A water ladder from buckets to a well, hand pump, solar pump, cistern and drip lines.
- Weather: rain, clouds and wind with a forecast, and rain you can collect.
- Animals: chickens with a coop and eggs, goats with milk and cheese, a pen, hay and a trailer.
- The auto-harvester and a machine framework, plus a cat that catches the mice that raid your food.
- Growing-season threats that escalate each year, with countermeasures you can buy.

### The idle routine
- A priority list of chores you can reorder and toggle, with optional conditions per chore.
- It plans a starter field when nothing is planned, resumes after sleep, and nudges you if it has nothing to do.

### Tutorial
- Short, skippable tip cards appear the first time something matters, and you can replay them from the Tips list.

## Improvements
- Storage never destroys goods: work waits until there is room, and the HUD shows what is full.
- Spoilage is visible, with warnings before food rots and a "won't ripen before winter" warning when planting.
- Survival warnings for food, firewood and condition, a bedtime notice, a morning report, and a winter readiness card.
- The Pantry scrolls, and the HUD wraps at phone widths.

## Visual and atmosphere
- Pixel placeholder art with a day-night grade and seasonal lighting.
- A world that responds to weather and the seasons.

## Bug fixes
- Waking up no longer ends the routine, and menus no longer leave you stuck idle for hours.
- The routine no longer starves an unattended start; it plans a starter field.
- Goods are no longer lost when a store is full.
- Several HUD overflow and overlap problems at phone width are fixed.
- Save slots load faster, and saves keep a backup.

## Under the hood
- Headless test suite, automated tests on every push, and a balance harness that simulates whole runs.
- A screenshot driver for checking the interface at desktop and phone sizes.
- Builds for Windows, Linux, macOS, Android, iOS and the web are produced by the release workflow.

## Known issues
- No audio yet, and all art is placeholder.
- No settings menu or desktop widget mode.
- The first run is meant to fail, and balance is still rough, especially income and the first winter.
- Phone performance has not been measured.

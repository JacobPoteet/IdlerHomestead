# Idler Homestead v0.5.0 (pre-release)

The biggest update so far, in two parts. A new interface: a pixel font, frosted plates lit by the world, a tool wheel, two paper books and lenses. And a deeper homestead: you earn Footing by getting through the nights, and events, night visitors, soil and a busier market give a run more to plan for. You can ignore each new system for free, so an idle run still gets by.

> **Saves from 0.4.0 can't be loaded.** When you open an old slot, a prompt lets you delete it or keep it.

## Highlights
- **A new interface.** The bars are gone. Small plates float over the world, a hold-to-open wheel holds six tools, and the old panels live in two tabbed books.
- **Footing and the Tonight card.** Before dusk a card tells you whether tonight is covered and offers one-tap fixes. Five good nights in a row earn Footing.
- **Things happen.** Sprains, roof leaks, a lost hiker or a peddler come as cards with two options and a free way to ignore them. Raccoons, deer, a fox or a bear may visit at night.
- **Fields that remember.** Eleven crops with planting windows, soil that wears out and recovers, rotation, weeds, compost and fine-quality harvests.
- **A market that fills up.** Selling one good over and over lowers its price, and a contracts board pays for named orders.
- **Choose your next start.** After a failed run you pick one of three previewed plots and a starting kit.

## New features
### Interface
- **The resting HUD.** Clock and vitals sit in the top corners, and stamps on the left edge show what waits on you, such as a delivery or an uncovered winter.
- **The tool wheel.** Hold to open it, or press keys 1 to 6: Hand, Hoe, Pail, Axe, Rod and Hammer. With the Axe or Pail, drag a rectangle to mark trees to chop or crops to water. The marks stay drawn in pencil until the work is done.
- **The Hammer and the crate.** Delivered buildings wait in the crate until you place them from the Hammer's ring, which also moves, picks up and demolishes.
- **Two books.** The Notebook holds the Journal, Fish guide and Diary. The Ledger holds the Pantry, Routine, Catalog and Truck.
- **Lenses.** Power, Water and Soil lenses wash the world to a blueprint and paint one system.
- **Plans in pencil.** Planned tiles show a sketch of the ripe crop, in chalk at night.
- **Wardrobe.** Earn 26 garments by playing. Light clothes speed your work, heavy ones need less heat at night, and Auto can dress you by the forecast.
- **Sandbox slots.** When you name a new homestead you can pick Sandbox: everything is free and the homesteader cannot die.
- **Key rebinding** in Settings > Controls, plus a Progress panel (P), a status card (H) and the Tonight card (T).

### Footing, goals and the night
- **Drinking water.** You drink each day from the cistern, then from a canteen you fill at the creek. Dry days bring Thirst, which builds slowly and never kills at once.
- **The Tonight card** opens before dusk with wood, food, water and shelter. Its fixes order the work for you: chop trees, fish, fill the canteen.
- **Footing.** Earn it with five good nights, slip it when wood or food runs short, win it back with two. It opens the contracts board.
- **A goal line** under the clock opens the Progress panel: milestones and what limits you most.
- **The Great Winter.** Year 3's winter is the hardest yet. Survive it to earn Established.
- **Winter wood allowance.** Standing trees give a limited share of wood each winter, so stock up in autumn. Logs, brush and bought wood are unlimited, and you can build more than one woodshed.

### Events and statuses
- **Event cards.** Body, home, land and visitor events offer two options with cost and risk in plain words. Ignoring is free and costs at most a mild, short setback, and a card waits while you are away.
- **Statuses.** A sprain, fever or burn shows as a chip under your Condition, with the days left and what clears it. No status can kill you.
- **Standing orders** (Careful or Bold) answer a kind of event for you, and Techniques and the first-aid kit add better options.

### Night threats and guards
- **Night visitors.** Mice, the fox, raccoons, deer and rabbits, and the black bear give a sign at dusk and take a capped share, never the food you need for the next two days.
- **Weather nights.** A cold front can beat the forecast, and in winter your pipes can freeze until noon.
- **Guards.** A farm dog and a lantern lower the risk, and each threat has its own counter, such as a scarecrow or a bear box.

### Fields and soil
- **Nine new crops:** lettuce, bush beans, carrot, onion, garlic, squash, tomato, corn and herbs, plus berry canes and an apple tree that crop every year.
- **Soil.** A tile's fertility drops at harvest and recovers while it rests. Planting the same family twice raises pest pressure, and at full pressure blight can take a harvest.
- **Weeds and compost.** Weeds cut yields until the new Upkeep routine pulls them. A compost bin turns manure and scraps into food for worn tiles.
- **Fine harvests** from healthy soil sell for more.
- **Sprinklers** water a 5 by 5 patch early in a run, using more water than drip lines.

### Market and upgrades
- **Market saturation.** The truck and the stand share one town demand per good, and auto-load mixes goods to keep prices up. The last day of each season is market day.
- **Contracts.** Once you have Footing, the board posts three orders a week. Finished ones raise your pay, and a missed one lapses with no penalty.
- **Tiers I to III** for eleven families, from the rod and axe to the stove, coop and truck bed. Each tier replaces the one below.

### Between runs
- **Plot preview.** Choose from three plots rated for soil, water, wood and sun. A plot with a hazard comes with a small bonus.
- **Starting kits.** Angler, Gardener, Herder, Lumberjack and Tinkerer each trade a small penalty for a head start.
- **The recovery screen** shows what came at you, who got hurt and any new clothes. It now scrolls.

## Improvements
- **Placement:** the game refuses a building, move or order that would wall your homesteader or the bed off from the road, and says why.
- **Pantry and truck:** hold a stepper to repeat, faster the longer you hold.
- **Journal:** search by name (Ctrl+F), and big chapters scroll.
- **Camera:** the pan to a placement card glides and re-fits after you drag.
- **Message log** is saved with the run.
- **Vertical sync** is now Off, On or Adaptive.
- **Right click** clears the selected tile.
- **First-run tips** cover the new systems.

## Visual and atmosphere
- Sun shadows that follow the day, night lights that light their surroundings, bloom, creek reflections, sunbeams and leaf dapple.
- Warmer art with tinted shadows, sun-lit edges and clumped trees.
- More varied land: a tributary, ponds and marshes, and oak, pine and birch woods, meadows and rocky ground.
- Wet objects darken in rain and dry over time, and frost reads clearly on snow.
- A new chibi homesteader who holds the tool for the job.
- Optional depth of field in Settings > Visuals.
- Panels dissolve in and out. Reduce motion swaps the dissolve for a two-step change.

## Bug fixes
- Plain vsync no longer flickers black on some Windows drivers.
- Shadows no longer leave a darker band where two of them overlap.
- Heat shimmer no longer crawls as scan lines.
- The water gauge no longer sticks over the homesteader after you switch tools.
- The ground no longer looks blotchy.
- A refused tap names the real reason instead of "Can't place it there".

## Under the hood
- GDD decisions D117 through D205. The save version moved from 8 to 33.
- The test suite runs in parallel and covers about 1,570 checks.
- The balance harness reports Footing and death spirals, plays a human-like policy and values sales at what they paid. The screenshot driver covers the new screens and runs in parallel.
- Releases now wait for the tests, format and lint checks. Tags are now `vX.Y.Z`.

## Known issues
- Balance is untuned. Every number for the new systems is a first guess, and the full pass is the next milestone (#396).
- Phone performance is unmeasured, including the lens pass (#315).
- Every sound is a placeholder (#243, #244).
- Blight, pest pressure and weeds show no cue on the field outside the Soil lens and tile card (#423, #427).
- Straw mulch unlocks through the Dry spell note, so weeds alone never teach it (#426).
- The routine seldom spreads compost while a weed sits nearer (#463).
- Recovery-screen thumbnails of new clothes are hard to tell apart (#333).
- The homesteader has no tool swing or walk frames yet (#335).
- Routine "If" conditions are switched off for this playtest (#337, #338).
- The `purchase` driver scenario fails at desktop size (#461).

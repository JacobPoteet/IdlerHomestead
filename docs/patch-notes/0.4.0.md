# Idler Homestead 0.4.0 (pre-release)

A playtest-feedback update: clearer building and buying rules, an easier way to replace and remove things, a top bar that tells you what is going on without opening anything, and a wider world.

Saves from 0.3.0 still load. New runs use the wider world, and existing saves keep their old map size.

## Highlights
- **One of each, and real upgrades.** The camp upgrades into the shack by replacing it, the roadside stand is a single building that visibly grows as you buy its upgrades, and shop items you already own say "Owned".
- **Replace and remove with less fuss.** Planning a crop over an existing one replaces it, Untill pulls a crop, and most placed things have a Destroy button.
- **A top bar you can read at a glance.** Each chip shows its state in the icon (a bar, a warning mark, a trend arrow), and the pop-ups are a few short lines.
- **Know what you are buying.** Tap or hold a shop item for a card that says what it does, what it needs and what it replaces.
- **A wider world.** The map is bigger, so zooming all the way out no longer shows black bars on wide screens.

## New features
### Building and buying
- **Camp to shack.** Buying the shack puts it in placement mode. With a camp standing, you place the shack over the camp. The ghost is labelled "replaces camp", the camp's footprint is outlined, and the confirm card says the camp is removed and nothing is refunded. Cancelling keeps the camp. The shack costs only its own price.
- **Owned and limits.** Unique items (roadside stand, woodshed, wood stove, electric heater, chest freezer, smoker, cheese press, goat trailer, shelter cat, camp, shack, rods, axe and the stand upgrades) can be bought once and then show "Owned". Animal pens, cisterns, power items, pumps, drip lines and auto-harvesters stay repeatable.
- **Tool upgrades replace the old tier.** The sturdy rod replaces the basic rod, and the big bucket replaces the plain bucket.
- **Roadside stand tiers.** The painted sign, the cooler and the display rack each change how the stand looks, and the stand's card lists what it has and what you can add.
- **Item cards in the shop.** Tap, long-press or right-click a Catalog or town shop row to see cost, what it does, what it gives, what it needs, what it replaces and why you can't buy it yet.
- **Range preview.** While placing an item with a range (the auto-harvester and the drip line), you see its range, the dimmed ranges of the same kind you already placed, and any overlap.

### Crops and clearing
- **Planning replaces crops.** Plan a field with a crop selected and every planned tile is replaced, even tiles already growing that crop. The preview marks tiles that will be replaced, and one confirm tells you how many crops are pulled and how many were ripe. Nothing is refunded. The idle routine never does this by itself.
- **Untill pulls a crop** and leaves tilled soil. No refund.
- **Destroy.** Any placed object and any growing crop has a Destroy button on its card, with a confirmation that says what is lost. The truck and a bed someone is sleeping in cannot be destroyed.
- **Clear trees** is a new routine category (off by default) that fells trees when your wood is below the level you set.

## Improvements
- **Top bar:** food, health, truck charge, wood, cistern and animals show a fill bar. Amber and red states add `!` and `!!` so colour is not the only signal. The truck and cistern show whether they are charging or filling. A new weather chip shows today and the next three days, with a mark for storms, threats and cold snaps.
- **Top-bar pop-ups** are a headline, up to four labelled rows, an optional one-line reason and at most one action.
- **UI size** now starts at 2x for new settings. Saved choices are kept, and phones still start at 1x.
- **Effects at 4x speed** now move at the 2x rate, so flames, wind and mist no longer rush.
- **Wider world:** the map is 128 by 80 tiles. The road now runs in from a shorter distance so water is still a short walk.

## Visual and atmosphere
- Placeholder icons for the weather chip and the stand upgrades.
- Range outlines and a red replace marker on planned tiles.

## Bug fixes
- **Broken art on launch.** Ground, trees, the truck and every icon could come out blank when the game reopened at a remembered window size that differed from the default. The sprite atlas is now built after the window is restored.
- Untill now works on a crop tile.

## Removed
- The skip night option.

## Known issues
- The weather icons and stand upgrade overlays are placeholders (#107).
- On touch, tapping an open tile still places a ranged item straight away, so the range preview only shows while a finger is held (#269).
- The chip row wraps to a second line on phones once a cistern, coop and pen are placed (#273).
- The Journal map's duplicate shop view has no item card (#270), and Clear trees has no explanation line in the routine panel (#271).
- Only the auto-harvester, smoker and axe have written shop descriptions. The rest show their one-line text.
- Threat gear is still one-per-run and will be revisited with the threat system (#274).
- Two driver scenarios, `clock` and `thresholds`, still fail (#250).
- All sounds are placeholders, and phone performance is unmeasured.
- Wood balance is untuned (#251).
